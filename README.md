# UnderScope

### See what the market is missing.

UnderScope is an AI-powered financial research and investigation platform designed to help investors look beyond stock prices and discover the hidden factors that can influence a company.

Instead of simply answering *"What happened?"*, UnderScope asks:

> **"What might I be missing?"**

It combines company fundamentals, investigative insights, dependency mapping, scenario analysis, and an AI research assistant into a single research experience.

---

## ✦ Why UnderScope?

Traditional financial dashboards are excellent at showing what has already happened:

- Stock price
- Revenue
- Earnings
- Valuation
- News

But understanding a company often requires looking beyond those numbers.

A company can be affected by:

**Suppliers → Customers → Commodities → Currencies → Regulation → Competitors → Macro conditions**

UnderScope connects these relationships and helps users investigate potential second-order effects.

## 🔎 Core Features

### Company Intelligence

Explore companies through a unified research interface covering:

- Company overview
- Financial metrics
- News
- Events
- Investigations
- Dependencies
- Scenario analysis

### 🕵️ UnderScope Investigations

Surface potential:

- Hidden risks
- Business dependencies
- Contradictions
- Second-order effects
- Areas requiring further investigation

Each investigation is presented with supporting context, potential impact, confidence, and evidence.

### 🕸️ Dependency Network

Explore relationships between a company and its:

- Suppliers
- Customers
- Competitors
- Commodities
- Countries
- Currencies
- Regulatory factors
- Infrastructure

Clicking a dependency reveals its relationship with the company and why it matters.
